Can you sit Step 1?

Indian MBBS students and graduates register through FSMB. ECFMG decides if you are eligible.

Your college is listed

It must appear in the World Directory with an ECFMG Sponsor Note, and your graduation year must read “Current”. No note means not eligible.

ECFMG bookletWorld Directory

Enrolled or graduated

You must be an enrolled student or a graduate when you apply and again on exam day. Dismissed or withdrawn students are not eligible.

USMLE bulletin

Basic sciences done

You must have finished the basic medical science part of the course by the start of your exam eligibility period. Your college official certifies this to ECFMG.

ECFMG booklet

Identity and application accepted

Identity verified, and an accepted Application for ECFMG Certification. Name, date of birth and gender must match your passport.

ECFMG booklet
Eligibility periods run in 3-month blocks Max 3 attempts of a Step in 12 months 4 failed attempts: no more Steps

Step 1

Can you sit Step 1?

Indian MBBS students and graduates register through FSMB. ECFMG decides if you are eligible.

Your college is listed

It must appear in the World Directory with an ECFMG Sponsor Note, and your graduation year must read “Current”. No note means not eligible.

ECFMG bookletWorld Directory

Enrolled or graduated

You must be an enrolled student or a graduate when you apply and again on exam day. Dismissed or withdrawn students are not eligible.

USMLE bulletin

Basic sciences done

You must have finished the basic medical science part of the course by the start of your exam eligibility period. Your college official certifies this to ECFMG.

ECFMG booklet

Identity and application accepted

Identity verified, and an accepted Application for ECFMG Certification. Name, date of birth and gender must match your passport.

ECFMG booklet
Eligibility periods run in 3-month blocks Max 3 attempts of a Step in 12 months 4 failed attempts: no more Steps

Step 2 CK

Step 2 CK: who can sit it, and the cost

Step 2 CK and Step 1 together are ECFMG’s medical science requirement. The rules for who can apply are the same as Step 1.

Same eligibility as Step 1

You must be an enrolled student or a graduate of a World Directory school with an ECFMG Sponsor Note, both when you apply and on exam day.

USMLE bulletin

Seven-year clock

Once you pass one exam, you have seven years to meet the other exam requirements for ECFMG Certification. ECFMG will not warn you if time runs out.

Step 3

Step 3: who can sit it, and the cost

Step 3 comes after you hold ECFMG Certification, so it is the latest part of the journey.

Pass Steps 1 and 2 CK

You need passing scores on both before you can apply for Step 3.

USMLE bulletin

Hold ECFMG Certification

A valid, unexpired ECFMG Certificate is required for graduates of schools outside the US.

USMLE apply pageUSMLE bulletin

One year of training advised

USMLE recommends at least one postgraduate training year in an accredited US programme, or being near completion of it. It is advice, not a rule.

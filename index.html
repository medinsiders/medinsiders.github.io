Frequently asked questions.

Why does Medinsider exist?

Most of us hear about scholarships, internships and research opportunities very late, sometimes six months or a year after the right time to apply. Often it depends on which city you are from or which college you study in.

Students at premier institutes face this too. Our founder came to know about many of these opportunities only towards the end of his third year, and it made him ask why access to information is so unequal.

Why is this information so hard to find?

Many government and private scholarships do not invest in outreach or awareness. So the information moves around in small closed groups instead of reaching every deserving student.

Social media is full of "How to get into XYZ institute" videos, but it rarely explains the bigger picture of research, innovation and healthcare entrepreneurship.

What does Medinsider help with?

We break down each opportunity, and we run webinars on the things students are usually unsure about:

  • How to write a strong Statement of Purpose
  • How to structure an effective CV
  • What selectors actually look for in an application

None of this is taught in our curriculum. We are usually told only to study well, prepare for exams and rely on coaching platforms.

What is our vision?

India needs more healthcare researchers, innovators and entrepreneurs. We believe everyone deserves equal access to opportunities, whatever their:

  • College
  • City
  • Background
  • Year of study

Our goal is to democratise access to information, so that students can make informed decisions early instead of discovering opportunities too late.

Is it paid?

No. Everything here is free. Share it with your friends.

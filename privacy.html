Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Medinsider is a free, student-led website for medical students. We collect as little as we can. We do not sell your data, show ads or use tracking analytics.

What we collect and why

When you…We collectWhy
Just browse the siteNothing ourselves. Our host (GitHub Pages) and Google Fonts receive your IP address, as every website host does.To deliver the pages
Suggest an internship or conferenceThe details you type, and your email only if you choose to give itTo check and publish the opportunity; to ask you a question about it
Apply to join our teamName, college, year, role, why you are interested, your CV file and emailTo select volunteers
Sign in to review a research groupYour Google name and email, college and yearTo confirm you are a real student and stop fake reviews
Verify with a college ID cardA photo of your ID cardTo confirm you are a student. We delete the photo within an hour of checking it.
Write a reviewYour rating and text. Your name is shown only if you tick "Show my name"; otherwise only your college and yearTo help other students compare groups
List a research groupYour name, role, institution, LinkedIn, Google Scholar and PubMed links, and group detailsTo check that mentors are genuine before listing them

Where it is stored

Everything you send is stored in a private Google Sheet and Google Drive folder that only the Medinsider team can open. Google processes this data for us. Your email, ID card and private notes are never shown on the website.

YouTube and Instagram

Our videos and reels come from YouTube and Instagram. They load only when you press play, and only then can those companies set their own cookies under their own privacy policies.

Cookies

Medinsider itself sets no cookies. If you sign in with Google, Google sets the cookies it needs for sign-in.

How long we keep it

  • ID card photos: deleted within an hour of being checked.
  • Reviews, listings and suggestions: kept while they are on the site, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Team applications: deleted within six months of the selection.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete any data we hold about you, or withdraw your consent. Email pk10171729@gmail.com from the address you used, and we will reply within 30 days.

Age

Medinsider is meant for medical students and doctors. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before sending us any information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
